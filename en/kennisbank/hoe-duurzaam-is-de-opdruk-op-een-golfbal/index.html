Printing & technique

How durable is the print on a printed golf ball?

A frequently asked question: won't the logo come straight off when you hit the ball? Here you will read how the print holds up and what you can expect from it.

Not a sticker, but a real print

The printing is applied professionally onto the original branded ball — it is emphatically not a sticker. As a result, the print holds up well through normal use on the course. exact wear resistance to be confirmed by client

Just play with it

You play with a printed golf ball exactly as you would with an unprinted one: the same ball, the same properties, now with your logo, name or text. As far as playing characteristics go, the print makes no difference.

What it depends on

How long a print stays looking crisp depends, among other things, on how often the ball is played and under what conditions. For corporate gifts and golf days — where the ball is often cherished more than it is worn out — the result stays looking good for a long time.

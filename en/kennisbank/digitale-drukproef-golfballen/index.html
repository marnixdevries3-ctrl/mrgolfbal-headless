Printing & technique

The digital proof: know exactly how it will look in advance

Nobody wants to receive a box of golf balls where the logo sits just a little differently than intended. That is why you always receive a digital proof from us first. So you know exactly what you are getting — before a single ball is printed.

What is a digital proof?

A digital proof is a visual representation of your logo or text on the chosen golf ball. You see the position, the colours and the proportions. It is your final check: is the colour right, is the logo positioned well, is the text spelled correctly?

How the process works

You start with a request via the configurator or the quote form: you choose the brand, model and quantity and supply your logo or text. We then create the digital proof and send it to you, together with a tailored quote. Production only starts after you have approved the proof. After that, allow a delivery time of 5 to 15 working days.

Why this matters

Printed golf balls are made to order. A good proof prevents disappointment and ensures the end result matches your expectation. It also leaves room to adjust small things: fine-tuning a colour, moving the logo slightly or correcting the text.

Handy to know

The online preview in the configurator is indicative: we always confirm the final position, colour and size in the digital proof. Not sure about the file you have supplied? See how to supply your logo, or ask Mark.

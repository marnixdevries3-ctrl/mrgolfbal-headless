Printing & technique

How many golf balls are in a dozen, sleeve or box?

Golf balls are sold in fixed units. Here are the numbers at a glance, so you can easily estimate your order.

The common units

A sleeve usually contains 3 golf balls. A dozen is 12 golf balls (usually 4 sleeves). A box can vary, but often contains several dozen. With us the minimum order is 144 golf balls — which works out at 12 dozen.

Handy when ordering

Say you want to give every participant at a golf day a sleeve of 3, then you calculate: number of participants × 3. If that leaves you under 144, order a little extra or opt for a bigger gift per person.

Work it out yourself

In the configurator you can see straight away how many dozen your quantity is. Not sure about the right quantity for an event? Read golf balls for your corporate golf day.

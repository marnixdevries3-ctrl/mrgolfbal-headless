Printing & technique

Printing golf balls in small quantities

Not everyone needs thousands of balls. So how do smaller quantities of printed golf balls work? These are the options.

Minimum order

The minimum order is 144 golf balls (for example 6 boxes of 24 or 12 dozen). That lower limit is deliberate: printing requires preparation, a print proof and set-up time, and below a certain number the cost per ball becomes disproportionately high.

And if you need fewer?

Do you genuinely need fewer? Then get in touch. Mark will look at what is possible and think along with you about a suitable solution — sometimes a slightly different model or quantity is the key.

Larger quantities are better value

Above 144 balls the price per ball becomes more favourable. For golf days, clubs and corporate gifts you are therefore often sharper on price with a larger order. You always receive a tailored quote.

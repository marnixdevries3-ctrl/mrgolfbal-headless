Applications & inspiration

Golf balls for your corporate golf day

A corporate golf day is the perfect moment to make your brand visible. Printed golf balls with your company logo give the day a polished, professional look — and participants take them home.

Decide on your quantity

Work out how many participants you expect and how many balls you want to give each person (a sleeve of three or a dozen is common). The minimum order is 144 golf balls; larger quantities are better value. Order a little generously rather than too tight.

Choose the right ball

For a premium experience, choose a Titleist; for larger groups at a keen price, a Pinnacle is ideal. Not sure? The golf ball selector gives tailored advice.

Think about the timing

Allow for both the digital proof and production. After approval of the proof, the delivery time is 5–15 working days. So plan your request well before the golf day, especially in the busy spring and summer.

How to request it

Fill in the business quote form with your preferred brand, model, quantity and delivery date, and upload your logo. You will receive a tailored quote and a proof. You will find more inspiration on golf balls for business.

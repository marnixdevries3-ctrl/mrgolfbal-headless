Applications & inspiration

Golf balls with a club logo for your golf club or shop

Golf balls carrying your own club logo give your club a polished, recognisable image — in the shop, at member promotions or as a welcome gift for new members.

What you use them for

Think of sales in the club shop, a gift with a new membership, prizes at competitions or a small present at a club event. A ball with a club logo stays in circulation and keeps your club visible.

Which ball do you choose?

For the shop a recognisable brand is nice: a Titleist for the premium customer, a Pinnacle for the keener segment. That way you serve different members.

How to arrange it

Supply your club logo (preferably as a vector file), receive a digital print proof and, once approved, production follows. From 144 balls; larger quantities are better value. More at for golf clubs & tournaments.

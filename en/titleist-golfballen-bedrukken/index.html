Why Titleist has been the most-played ball on tour for decades

When you give away a printed golf ball, you give more than a gift: you give a brand that is recognised instantly on the course. Titleist built that reputation through a relentless focus on one thing — a ball that behaves the same way on every shot. From the opening drive to the final putt, a serious golfer wants predictability, and it is precisely that reliability that has kept Titleist the most-played ball in the bags of tour professionals worldwide for generations.

For your logo, that matters in a concrete way. A recipient who recognises the Pro V1 or Pro V1x subconsciously links your company or club name to quality and care. That is exactly the association you want from a corporate gift. It is a subtle but powerful signal: giving away a tour ball shows you consider the relationship worth the best.

That status is also why golf clubs, resorts and companies are keen to put their logo on a Titleist rather than on a nameless ball. The name carries years of credibility that you cannot fake in a print — you simply borrow it. Not sure which model suits your audience best? The golf ball selector guides you in a few steps, or put your question straight to us via contact.

Model by model: how each Titleist takes a print

Every Titleist model we personalise has a smooth, even surface that lends itself perfectly to a crisp print in one to five spot colours. Even so, each model has its own character worth weighing up.

  • Pro V1 — the flagship. Its soft cast-urethane cover gives a refined, matt white on which brand colours read calmly and look premium. Ideal when your logo should feel subtle yet high-end.
  • Pro V1x — slightly firmer with a higher flight. The same clean printability as the Pro V1; choosing between the two is about feel, not print quality.
  • TruFeel — the keenest-priced Titleist. Perfect when you need a large number of personalised balls but still want the Titleist name. A favoured choice for golf days with many players.
  • AVX — soft feel with a lower flight and spin. A distinctive gift for the golfer who wants something a little different from the standard.
  • Velocity — built for speed and distance, with bright, eye-catching base finishes that make your logo pop.

Whichever model you choose, we show you a digital proof first, so you see exactly how your logo lands on that specific ball before anything is printed.

Which Titleist suits which business goal?

Choosing the right ball is less about finding "the best" and more about finding "the best for this purpose". A few guidelines we apply in practice:

  • Premium corporate gift for a select group — choose the Pro V1 or Pro V1x. The recipient can tell you did not cut corners, and that reflects well on the relationship.
  • Large company golf day with mixed ability — the TruFeel offers the Titleist brand value at a price that works well in higher volumes.
  • Distinctive gift for the connoisseur — the AVX or Velocity stands out among the usual sleeves and shows you have given it thought.

Want to match this to your budget and the number of recipients? Explore the wider options under applications or ask Mark for advice. As a PGA professional he knows both the ball characteristics and the expectations of golfing recipients.

Logo placement and alignment on a premium ball

On an expensive ball you want the logo to be right to the millimetre. Golf balls are usually printed around the side (next to the maker's mark) or on the pole. A clean, symmetrical logo gives a tidy result; for a wide or unusually shaped logo we look together at the best scale and position so it does not disappear into the dimples.

A few practical points that make the difference: supply your logo as a vector file for razor-sharp edges, provide your brand colours as PMS/Pantone values, and bear in mind the existing Titleist maker's mark that stays on the ball. You can read more about supplying artwork correctly in our knowledge base under supplying your logo and how many colours you are best off using.

Presentation and etiquette: giving a printed Titleist well

A golf ball is small, but the way you hand it over shapes the impression. Titleist balls come in tidy dozen packs and sleeves of three, which makes them suitable as a standing gift on a desk or as a give-away at a dinner. For a corporate gift a dozen often works better than loose balls: it feels complete and ready to use.

When giving them away, mind the recipient's level. A keen single-figure handicapper values a Pro V1 more than anyone; an occasional player is just as pleased with a TruFeel and barely notices the difference on the course. A dozen that matches how someone actually plays always lands better than the most expensive model left sitting in a cupboard. FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order?
The minimum order is 144 golf balls, for example 6 boxes of 24 or 12 dozen. Larger quantities earn tiered discounts.
Do I get a digital proof first?
Yes, always. You receive a digital proof of your logo on the ball and production only starts after your approval.
How long does delivery take?
Allow 5 to 15 working days after you approve the digital proof.
How many colours can I print?
Golf balls are printed in 1 to 5 spot colours. Provide your brand colours as PMS/Pantone values for the best match.
How do I supply my logo?
Preferably as a vector file (EPS, AI or PDF) so the logo comes out perfectly crisp. A high-resolution PNG can work for simple logos.
Can I play with printed golf balls?
Yes. They are original branded balls with a professional print, so they play exactly like unprinted balls.

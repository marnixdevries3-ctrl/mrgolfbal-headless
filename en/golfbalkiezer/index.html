FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the golf ball finder work?
You answer 10 short questions about your goal, level, budget and preferences. Based on your answers we score every ball and show the three best matches.
Is the advice binding?
No, it is indicative. The finder points you in the right direction; for a tailored recommendation you can always ask Mark, a PGA professional.
Can I have the recommended ball printed?
Yes. Every recommended model can be printed with your logo or text. Open the configurator to start.
What if I am not sure about my answers?
Just pick what fits best. You can go back a step at any time, or start again at the end.

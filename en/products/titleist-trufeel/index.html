Who the printed TruFeel is the smartest choice for

The TruFeel is the Titleist for the big gesture. When you need a large quantity of logo golf balls — for a well-attended golf day, a sponsor package, a goodie bag or a broad relations base — you want brand value without the price per ball getting out of hand. The TruFeel offers exactly that balance: the trusted Titleist name at the keenest price in the range, so your logo lands on a genuine brand ball rather than an anonymous budget one.

That makes the TruFeel ideally suited to orders with many recipients of mixed ability. Everyone gets a recognisable Titleist in hand, from the keen club golfer to the occasional player. To see how this fits a large event, look at the company golf day, or match the quantity with the ball selector.

Extra-soft feel: what it means for the recipient

The TruFeel is known for its extra-soft feel, with a mid-high ball flight and balanced spin. For the broad group of recipients that is exactly the appeal: a soft ball feels comfortable and forgiving when putting and chipping, without the exacting demands a tour ball places on the strike. Recreational and average players often get more enjoyment out of it than from an uncompromising premium ball.

On longer shots too, the TruFeel does what most golfers are after: a smooth feel and a predictable, straight flight. It is a ball that works with you rather than punishing you — and that makes it a gift that goes down well with a wide audience. For those who prefer a firmer, higher-flying ball, the Pro V1x or the premium Pro V1 are the alternatives.

Why the TruFeel is a strong gift for large runs

At higher quantities every euro per ball counts, yet you do not want to give up on impression. That is where the TruFeel's strength as a branded gift lies: the recipient sees the Titleist name, not the price tag. For the giver it means a favourable unit price that lets you reach more people without putting your logo on a ball that feels cheap.

That makes the TruFeel ideal for applications where reach matters more than exclusivity: participant gifts, trade-show giveaways for golfing relations or a large seasonal campaign. And because the tiered price improves at higher quantities, it pays to estimate your volume well in advance. See the wider options at golf balls as a corporate gift, or start the configurator for a free proof and quote.

How your logo comes into its own on the TruFeel

The TruFeel has a clean, even white surface on which a print in one to five spot colours comes out clear and sharp. For a logo that has to look consistent across large quantities, that is a real plus: every ball in the run shows your branding in the same tidy way, alongside the existing Titleist branding that stays on the ball.

Even at high volume we show a digital proof beforehand, so you can see exactly how your logo sits on the TruFeel before the whole run goes into production. Supply your design as a vector file and provide your colours as PMS/Pantone values for the best match across the batch. Read more about printing original Titleist balls at printing Titleist golf balls.

Corporate and club use the TruFeel suits best

The TruFeel is the workhorse ball for applications at scale. Golf clubs use it as a participant gift at open tournaments, companies use it for large golf days and trade-show campaigns, and organisers drop it into goodie bags where a genuine brand ball makes the difference. Wherever you want to reach many people with a recognisable Titleist, the TruFeel is the logical choice. For recurring campaigns too, such as an annual tournament or a fixed seasonal gift, it is a ball that lets you carry your brand consistently and affordably year after year.

For a mixed field, organisers often pair the TruFeel with a premium model for the guests of honour, so every budget and level is served. If you would like to work out that split for your company golf day, Mark is glad to help as a PGA professional via contact. FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order?
The minimum order is 144 golf balls, for example 6 boxes of 24 or 12 dozen. Larger quantities earn tiered discounts.
Do I get a digital proof first?
Yes, always. You receive a digital proof of your logo on the ball and production only starts after your approval.
How long does delivery take?
Allow 5 to 15 working days after you approve the digital proof.
How many colours can I print?
Golf balls are printed in 1 to 5 spot colours. Provide your brand colours as PMS/Pantone values for the best match.
How do I supply my logo?
Preferably as a vector file (EPS, AI or PDF) so the logo comes out perfectly crisp. A high-resolution PNG can work for simple logos.
Can I play with printed golf balls?
Yes. They are original branded balls with a professional print, so they play exactly like unprinted balls.

Who the printed Pro V1x works best for

The Pro V1x is the tour ball for the player who wants to attack with height and spin. As a corporate gift it targets a specific type of recipient: the keen golfer with a low to mid handicap who likes their approaches to land steeply and stop quickly. Where the Pro V1 serves the broad premium spectrum, the Pro V1x appeals to the player who deliberately chooses extra height and control — and genuinely feels the difference.

That makes the Pro V1x a strong gift when you know your recipients are serious golfers: a members' tournament at a good club, a select group of stronger players or a sponsor flight full of low handicappers. If you are not sure which profile you are dealing with, the ball selector will help, or put your question to us via contact.

Height and spin: what the recipient gains

The Pro V1x feels slightly firmer than the Pro V1 and flies higher, with very high spin around the green. For the recipient, that higher flight means approaches come in more steeply and stop faster on the green — exactly what an attacking player wants at tucked pins. The firmer response also gives a slightly crisper feel at impact, which many better players experience as direct and controllable.

The high spin is a double-edged sword that comes into its own in skilled hands: strike the ball well and you get maximum grip and stopping power in return. That makes the Pro V1x a gift that rewards the recipient's ability rather than forgives it. For a player who takes their game seriously, that is a compliment in itself: you are giving them a ball built to be exploited. To see exactly how this compares with the softer, penetrating Pro V1, read Pro V1 vs Pro V1x.

Why the Pro V1x is a considered branded gift

A gift makes the biggest impression when it shows you have thought about the recipient. By deliberately choosing the Pro V1x rather than defaulting to the Pro V1, you send a signal: you know this player values height and spin. For the golfer who fits that profile exactly, it feels like a gift tailored to them, not a standard pick.

What is more, the Pro V1x carries the same tour status and premium look as the Pro V1 — it is not a compromise but an equal flagship with a different character. With your logo on it, it becomes a corporate gift that conveys both quality and genuine know-how. See the wider uses at golf balls as a corporate gift, or start the configurator straight away for a free proof.

How your logo looks on the Pro V1x

In terms of printability the Pro V1x is a twin of the Pro V1: the same clean, even urethane surface on which a print in one to five spot colours turns out sharp and tidy. The choice between the two is therefore about feel, not print quality — your logo comes out just as beautifully on both models, alongside the existing Titleist branding.

As with every premium model, we show a digital proof beforehand so you can see exactly how your logo sits on this ball before anything is printed. Supply your design as a vector file and provide brand colours as PMS/Pantone values for the sharpest match. Read more about the options at printing Titleist golf balls, or compare the softer alternative on Titleist Pro V1.

Corporate and club use that suits the Pro V1x

The Pro V1x is at its strongest in applications with an accomplished golfing audience. Think of the prize table at a club championship, a sponsor flight at an invitational, member kits for an ambitious club or a gift for the stronger corporate golf team. At a company golf day with many experienced players, the Pro V1x feels like the logical premium choice that matches their game.

For larger mixed fields, organisers often pair the Pro V1x with a more accessible model so every participant gets a ball suited to their level. If you would like to work out that split for your company golf day, Mark is glad to help as a PGA professional. Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order?
The minimum order is 144 golf balls, for example 6 boxes of 24 or 12 dozen. Larger quantities earn tiered discounts.
Do I get a digital proof first?
Yes, always. You receive a digital proof of your logo on the ball and production only starts after your approval.
How long does delivery take?
Allow 5 to 15 working days after you approve the digital proof.
How many colours can I print?
Golf balls are printed in 1 to 5 spot colours. Provide your brand colours as PMS/Pantone values for the best match.
How do I supply my logo?
Preferably as a vector file (EPS, AI or PDF) so the logo comes out perfectly crisp. A high-resolution PNG can work for simple logos.
Can I play with printed golf balls?
Yes. They are original branded balls with a professional print, so they play exactly like unprinted balls.

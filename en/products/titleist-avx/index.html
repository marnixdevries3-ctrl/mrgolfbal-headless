Who the printed AVX is the distinctive gift for

The AVX is the Titleist for the connoisseur. It is a premium ball that does something slightly different from the familiar tour models: very soft in feel, with a lower ball flight and less spin in the long game. As a corporate gift, the AVX targets the golfer who deliberately steps away from the standard — someone who already knows the Pro V1 and appreciates something a little different. To that recipient, a printed AVX feels like a gift chosen with genuine know-how.

That makes the AVX ideal when you want to stand out from the usual sleeves. Where everyone gives a Pro V1, the AVX shows you have looked further. For a broader view of suitable moments, see golf balls as a corporate gift, or let the ball selector help you decide whether this character suits your audience.

Low flight and soft feel: what the recipient notices

The AVX combines a very soft feel with a lower, penetrating ball flight and less spin in the long game. For the recipient that translates into two things. First, an exceptionally soft feel when putting and chipping, which many players experience as comfortable and controlled. Second, a low, boring flight that performs especially well in wind — the ball stays low and stable where higher ones get picked up.

The lower spin in the long game also produces extra roll after landing, which players chasing distance can appreciate. It is a character that stands out precisely because it is so different from the high, spin-rich Pro V1x. To see those differences side by side, compare with the Pro V1x and the classic Pro V1.

Why the AVX is a distinctive branded gift

A gift that stands out stays in mind longer. Precisely because the AVX is given away less often than the Pro V1, it lends your logo a slightly more exclusive edge. The recipient who recognises the model knows this is a deliberate choice — not an automatic reach for the best-known ball. That makes the AVX a corporate gift that conveys both premium quality and a taste of its own.

For the giver that is exactly the effect you want with a select group who already know the usual gifts. The AVX says: I have thought about this, and I am giving you something special. Want to see how your logo sits on this ball? Start the configurator for a free digital proof, or put your question to us via contact.

How your logo looks on the AVX

The AVX has a clean, even urethane surface that lets a print in one to five spot colours come out sharp and tidy — the same high-quality printability you expect from a premium Titleist. On this distinctive ball, brand colours read as calm and high-end, alongside the existing Titleist branding that stays on the ball.

As with every premium model, we show a digital proof beforehand so you can see exactly how your logo sits on the AVX before anything is printed. Supply your design as a vector file and provide brand colours as PMS/Pantone values for the sharpest match. Read more about the options at printing Titleist golf balls.

Corporate and club use that suits the AVX

The AVX is at its best in applications where you want to stand out with a select group. Think of a gift for long-standing relations who already know the standard models, a prize at a closed tournament for stronger players, or a surprising element in a premium member kit. Golf clubs and resorts are glad to use the AVX as the slightly more special alternative to the usual tour ball. That very individuality makes it well suited to relations you want to feel they are not just another name on a list.

At a company golf day, the AVX suits the flight you want to give something extra, while you use a more accessible model for the wider field. If you would like to work out that split for your company golf day, Mark is glad to help as a PGA professional. FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order?
The minimum order is 144 golf balls, for example 6 boxes of 24 or 12 dozen. Larger quantities earn tiered discounts.
Do I get a digital proof first?
Yes, always. You receive a digital proof of your logo on the ball and production only starts after your approval.
How long does delivery take?
Allow 5 to 15 working days after you approve the digital proof.
How many colours can I print?
Golf balls are printed in 1 to 5 spot colours. Provide your brand colours as PMS/Pantone values for the best match.
How do I supply my logo?
Preferably as a vector file (EPS, AI or PDF) so the logo comes out perfectly crisp. A high-resolution PNG can work for simple logos.
Can I play with printed golf balls?
Yes. They are original branded balls with a professional print, so they play exactly like unprinted balls.

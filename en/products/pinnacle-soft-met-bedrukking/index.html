Who the printed Pinnacle Soft is the right choice for

The Pinnacle Soft is the ball for anyone who wants to print plenty of golf balls without giving up on enjoyment. With a soft feel at a keen price, this is the model of choice for large runs: golf days with dozens or hundreds of players, membership promotions at a golf club, and corporate gifts where the numbers add up. Where a tour ball becomes costly per unit, the Soft lets you hand out generously within the same budget.

The audience is above all the recreational golfer who enjoys a soft ball that runs away easily. For that player the Soft is just right: comfortable, forgiving and pleasant to play, without paying a premium price for characteristics the average golfer barely uses. Torn between the Soft and the firmer, distance-focused Pinnacle Rush? Read the comparison in our knowledge base under Pinnacle Soft vs Rush, or browse every printed Pinnacle model.

Playing characteristics explained: what "soft" means on the course

The word "soft" comes up a lot with golf balls, but what do you actually notice? For the Pinnacle Soft it is all about comfort and accessibility.

  • Soft feel — at impact, and especially around the green and on putts, the Soft feels pleasantly gentle. Many recreational players experience that as nicer and "easier" than a hard ball.
  • Mid-to-high flight — the ball gets up into the air readily without ballooning, which gives a natural, controllable trajectory for the average swing speed.
  • Lower spin with plenty of run-out — less spin means the ball rolls on well after landing. On the longer shots that adds yards, which is exactly what pleases the occasional golfer.

The overall picture is a relaxed, enjoyable ball that asks little of the player. That makes it ideal for events with a broad field, where nobody has to wonder whether the ball suits their level. Explore the options on the Pinnacle overview page.

Why the Soft is the go-to ball for golf days and large runs

At a golf day or membership event, it is not just the ball itself that counts but the sum underneath it. That is exactly where the Pinnacle Soft shines: the keen price makes it possible to give every player a sleeve or a dozen without the budget running away. For organisers who want to reach the largest possible group with a fixed amount, that is often the deciding factor.

On top of that, the Soft has a large, even surface that lends itself perfectly to a highly visible print. At a golf day you then see your logo go by dozens of times — on the tee, in the bag and afterwards in every player's cupboard. That is lasting visibility at a low cost per contact moment. If you want to use it for an event, take a look at golf balls for the corporate golf day or golf balls for a golf tournament. Ready to start? Open the configurator.

How your logo looks on the Pinnacle Soft

One of the practical strengths of the Pinnacle Soft is its generous, even print surface. That literally gives your logo room to breathe: even a larger or more detailed design comes across neatly, and text stays legible. We print in one to five spot colours, with your brand colours best supplied as PMS/Pantone values for a reliable match. to confirm

Because the Soft is often ordered in large quantities, consistency matters: every unit in the run receives the same crisp print. You receive a digital proof beforehand, so you approve the placement, scale and colours before the whole batch is produced. That way you can be sure the ball the hundredth player receives looks just as tidy as the first. Questions about supplying your logo? Get in touch via contact and we will look at it with you.

Best use for companies, clubs and events

The Pinnacle Soft comes into its own in situations where volume and price come together. A few applications we see often in practice:

  • Corporate golf days with many players — give everyone a printed ball without straining the budget. A favoured choice for corporate golf days.
  • Membership promotions and club sales — golf clubs are keen to have the Soft printed with the club logo as an affordable club ball or as a welcome gift for new members.
  • Corporate gifts in larger numbers — when you want to thank many contacts at once, the Soft offers brand value and a tidy print at a friendly price per unit.

Want to weigh the Soft against the distance-focused Rush, or against a premium Titleist? Use the golf ball selector, read Pinnacle Soft vs Rush, or ask Mark, our PGA professional, for advice via contact.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order?
The minimum order is 144 golf balls, for example 6 boxes of 24 or 12 dozen. Larger quantities earn tiered discounts.
Do I get a digital proof first?
Yes, always. You receive a digital proof of your logo on the ball and production only starts after your approval.
How long does delivery take?
Allow 5 to 15 working days after you approve the digital proof.
How many colours can I print?
Golf balls are printed in 1 to 5 spot colours. Provide your brand colours as PMS/Pantone values for the best match.
How do I supply my logo?
Preferably as a vector file (EPS, AI or PDF) so the logo comes out perfectly crisp. A high-resolution PNG can work for simple logos.
Can I play with printed golf balls?
Yes. They are original branded balls with a professional print, so they play exactly like unprinted balls.

Who the printed Pro V1 is the right choice for

The Pro V1 is the ball you give when the impression matters. Think of a select group of long-standing relations, a board, a small leadership team or the guests of honour at an anniversary — people for whom an average gift says too little. Because the Pro V1 has been the most-played ball on tour for years, a golfing recipient recognises the model instantly, and that recognition does the work: your logo lands on a ball that stands for the highest level of the game.

For the recipient the Pro V1 is not a ball that disappears into a drawer either. Serious players with a low to mid handicap genuinely play it, and every round they walk brings your logo back onto the course. For a broader view of suitable moments and audiences, see golf balls as a corporate gift, or use the ball selector to match your choice to the person in front of you.

What the playing characteristics mean for the recipient

The Pro V1's reputation rests on a combination that is hard to imitate: a soft feel at impact, a penetrating and consistent ball flight, and plenty of grip around the green. For the golfer who receives your ball, that translates into confidence. The soft cast-urethane cover gives a refined, controlled feel on short chips and putts, while on long shots the ball keeps cutting steadily through the wind.

That predictability is exactly what a better player values. They know what the ball will do, round after round, and subconsciously associate that trust with the name printed on it — yours. A gift that keeps performing on the course reinforces the message you want it to carry. To place its character alongside the firmer, higher-flying variant, read the comparison Pro V1 vs Pro V1x.

Why the Pro V1 makes a strong branded gift

A corporate gift works best when it is functional, long-lasting and carries positive associations. The printed Pro V1 ticks all three boxes. It genuinely gets used, lasts many holes and carries the status of a tour ball. Where a pen or a keyring quickly turns anonymous, a Pro V1 with your logo stays a deliberate choice the recipient notices.

There is a subtle psychology to it as well. Giving a tour ball says: this relationship is worth the very best to me. That signal is sincere and hard to misread. For a premium corporate gift you do not need to make it bigger than it is — the ball speaks for itself. Ready to see your logo on a Pro V1? How your logo comes into its own on the Pro V1

The Pro V1 has an even, matte-white surface that lets brand colours read as calm and high-end. Fine lines and small details stay legible because the print is applied in sharp spot colours, alongside the existing Titleist branding that remains on the ball. On a premium ball you want that alignment right to the millimetre, which is precisely why we show a digital proof beforehand.

For the best result, supply your logo as a vector file and provide your colours as PMS/Pantone values. With a wide or unusually shaped logo we look together at the ideal scale and position, so nothing disappears into the dimples. For more on what is possible when printing original Titleist balls, see printing Titleist golf balls.

Corporate and club use the Pro V1 suits best

Within an organisation the Pro V1 fits applications where exclusivity matters more than volume. Think of a closed invitational, a VIP flight at a larger event, a farewell gift for a valued partner or a modest run for your most important accounts. Golf clubs and resorts are glad to put their logo on the Pro V1 for top-tier member kits or as a prize at the club championship. The model underlines the standing of the club and gives members tangible proof of quality they are happy to carry onto the course.

If you are organising a company golf day with a mixed field, it can be smart to reserve the Pro V1 for the guests of honour and use a more affordable model for the wider group. Unsure about the split? As a PGA professional, Mark is glad to think it through with you via contact. FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order?
The minimum order is 144 golf balls, for example 6 boxes of 24 or 12 dozen. Larger quantities earn tiered discounts.
Do I get a digital proof first?
Yes, always. You receive a digital proof of your logo on the ball and production only starts after your approval.
How long does delivery take?
Allow 5 to 15 working days after you approve the digital proof.
How many colours can I print?
Golf balls are printed in 1 to 5 spot colours. Provide your brand colours as PMS/Pantone values for the best match.
How do I supply my logo?
Preferably as a vector file (EPS, AI or PDF) so the logo comes out perfectly crisp. A high-resolution PNG can work for simple logos.
Can I play with printed golf balls?
Yes. They are original branded balls with a professional print, so they play exactly like unprinted balls.

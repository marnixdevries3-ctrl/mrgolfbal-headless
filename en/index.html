Frequently asked questions

Golf ball printing — good to know

What is the minimum order?
The minimum order is 144 golf balls (for example 6 boxes of 24 or 12 dozen). Larger quantities are of course possible and earn a volume discount.
Do I see a proof first?
Yes. You always receive a digital proof of your logo or text. We only start production after your approval.
How long does delivery take?
Expect 5–15 working days after proof approval.
How do I supply my logo?
Preferably as a vector file (EPS, AI or PDF), so the logo comes out razor-sharp on the ball. Not sure about your file? Mark will help you with the layout.
Can I play with printed golf balls?
Yes. These are original brand balls with a professional print; you simply play with them.
Can I return printed golf balls?
Personalised/printed golf balls are made to order and cannot be returned. For non-printed items a 30-day return period applies.

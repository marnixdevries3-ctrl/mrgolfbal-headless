Corporate-gift strategy: the right moment and the right message

A printed golf ball works best when it lands at the right moment. Think of the start of the season, when golfers restock their bags and new balls are welcome; an anniversary or the close of a project; or a thank-you after winning a tender. The timing decides whether your gift feels thoughtful or like a random give-away.

The message around it amplifies the effect. A short, personal card with a dozen balls does more than a box with no context. Consider too whether you place your logo alone or add a name, a date or a short line of text for a more personal result — a ball carrying the recipient's initials feels like a gift made especially for them. Our golf balls as a corporate gift page sets out worked scenarios; if you are unsure about brand or model, Mark is glad to help via contact.

Brand visibility and return: where your logo ends up

Where many promotional items end up in a drawer, a golf ball goes out onto the course. There it is picked up repeatedly, teed up and seen by playing partners. Over a four-hour round, in the company of three or four other golfers, your logo passes by again and again — for the player and for the rest of the group. That is a form of visibility few other corporate gifts offer.

The context is favourable, too: golf is an environment of relaxation, networking and business. Your brand appears not as advertising but as a useful object in a positive setting. Where a banner or advert is clicked away or ignored, a golf ball is picked up and used — the recipient effectively invites your brand out onto the course.

Unlike many promotional items, a good golf ball also has a long useful life: it does not go in the bin after one use but keeps going round after round. As long as the ball is in play, so is your logo. If you want to combine that visibility with a sponsor activation or clinic, explore the wider applications or start designing straight away in the configurator.

Procurement and invoicing: handled properly, no online checkout

Business orders call for a business-like process. With us you do not pay online: you receive a tailored quote with a clear volume price, and once approved everything is settled neatly by invoice. That suits procurement processes where a purchase order, invoice address and payment term are part of how things are done.

Handy to have to hand when you make a request:

  • the preferred brand and model (or a request for advice);
  • the number of balls or boxes;
  • your logo, preferably as a vector file (EPS, AI or PDF);
  • the desired delivery date and the invoice address.

Repeat orders and stock management

Many companies do not order once but regularly — for recurring events, ongoing corporate gifts or a standing stock at the office. That makes a good repeat arrangement valuable. Because we keep your design and print specifications on file, a reorder in the same specification is usually quicker and simpler than the first.

A few tips to keep repeat orders running smoothly:

  • Order a little more generously than your immediate need, so you have a buffer for unexpected occasions.
  • Keep your brand files current and supply any logo changes in good time.
  • Plan ahead towards busy periods; allow 5 to 15 working days after approval of the proof.

If you want to set up a recurring arrangement, discuss it via contact. Exact agreements on stock and retention periods are recorded per client. to confirm

Combine with a company golf day or event

Printed golf balls come into their own as part of a bigger whole. Use them as a welcome gift on arrival, as a prize for a longest-drive or nearest-the-pin, or as a tangible keepsake players take home. That way the ball enhances the experience of the day and keeps your brand visible afterwards.

In practical terms it pays to plan the numbers generously: allow at least one sleeve per player, plus a reserve for the competition elements and no-shows. For ideas on shaping such a day, you will find concrete suggestions on our golf balls for the company golf day page. Ready to work through numbers and models? Request a proposal via the quote form.

Tax-friendly gifting: keep it clear

Corporate gifts and staff tokens are subject to tax rules — for instance around the deductibility of costs and, in the Netherlands, the work-related expenses scheme. The precise treatment depends on your situation, the amount and the recipient, and those rules also change from time to time.

Our advice is straightforward: keep your order and invoice properly documented so that your bookkeeping and accountant can work with them easily. We provide a clear invoice you can process without fuss. For the tax side itself we refer you to your own adviser or accountant — that is the only way to be sure what applies in your case. to confirm If you have questions about the order itself, we are glad to help via contact.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order?
The minimum order is 144 golf balls, for example 6 boxes of 24 or 12 dozen. Larger quantities earn tiered discounts.
Do I get a digital proof first?
Yes, always. You receive a digital proof of your logo on the ball and production only starts after your approval.
How long does delivery take?
Allow 5 to 15 working days after you approve the digital proof.
How many colours can I print?
Golf balls are printed in 1 to 5 spot colours. Provide your brand colours as PMS/Pantone values for the best match.
How do I supply my logo?
Preferably as a vector file (EPS, AI or PDF) so the logo comes out perfectly crisp. A high-resolution PNG can work for simple logos.
Can I play with printed golf balls?
Yes. They are original branded balls with a professional print, so they play exactly like unprinted balls.

The Pinnacle value proposition: a brand ball without the premium price tag

Not every goal calls for a tour ball. When you need many personalised golf balls — for a golf day, a clinic or a large give-away — what counts most is the balance between visibility and cost. Pinnacle is built for exactly that: a real, recognisable brand with a solid construction, at a price that lets you hand them out generously without overspending.

The clever part is that you keep the brand value. A recipient does not see an anonymous "promotional ball" but a genuine Pinnacle with your logo on it. That is the difference between a gift that vanishes into a bag and one that actually makes it onto the course. Pinnacle is, moreover, a name with history in golf, built around distance and enjoyment for the broad body of golfers — precisely the people you want to win over at a golf day or a sponsor activation.

A useful way to think about it is "cost per visible impression". Because a golf ball is picked up, teed and seen over several holes, you spread the relatively low unit cost of a Pinnacle across many moments of exposure. For a brand activation where reach matters, that is an attractive sum. Want to weigh Pinnacle against a premium Titleist for a tiered approach? Compare freely in the configurator or ask for advice via contact.

The economics of large runs: why volume works in your favour

Printing involves fixed set-up costs — think of setting up the artwork and producing the proof. Those costs spread across the number of balls you order. The larger the run, the smaller the share of set-up per ball, and so the keener the price per unit. That is precisely why Pinnacle comes into its own at higher volumes.

The minimum order is 144 golf balls (12 dozen), and from there volume discounts apply as your numbers grow. A few practical considerations:

  • Round your quantity up generously. If you sit just below a volume threshold, a slightly larger order is often barely more per ball — and you keep a handy stock.
  • Keep your print consistent. One design across the whole run keeps set-up efficient.
  • Plan a single print round. Ordering everything at once is better value than separate small reorders.

For the keenest price per ball at a specific quantity, simply request a tailored quote via the quote page.

Soft or Rush for your event? How to choose with purpose

Between them, the two Pinnacle models cover almost every event scenario. The choice depends on the format and the profile of your players.

  • Pinnacle Soft — soft feel, pleasant around the green and on the putting surface. The logical choice for a mixed field at a company golf day, where comfort and a refined feel matter more than maximum distance.
  • Pinnacle Rush — a firm core built for speed and distance. Ideal for a longest-drive contest, a tournament or players with a higher swing speed who enjoy roll.

Undecided? For a scramble or a networking day with many beginners you would usually choose Soft; for a competitive day with experienced players Rush adds a little extra excitement. The golf ball selector points you in the right direction quickly.

Why Pinnacle prints so well

A good print starts with a good surface. Pinnacle balls have a bright white, even finish with a generous, uniform area beside the maker's mark — exactly where your logo shows to best effect. That even white creates high contrast, so brand colours read fresh and saturated rather than dull.

We personalise only original Pinnacle balls in one to five spot colours, and you always see the result first on a digital proof. That way you can be sure the colours are right and the logo is sharp before the whole run is printed. On large runs that upfront check is worth its weight in gold: one approval on the proof and the entire order comes out consistently from the same specification. Want to know more about colour choice? Read how many colours you are best off using, and how best to supply your logo for a crisp result.

How durable does the print stay on the course?

A printed ball needs to still look smart after a few holes. The print is applied and bonded professionally to the ball surface so that it withstands normal play: contact with the club, the grass and the handling in and out of the bag. Under normal use, your logo stays clearly visible for the life of the ball.

As with any golf ball, wear from repeated hard contact, bunker sand or a meeting with a tree is part of the game and may mark the ball (and therefore the print) over time. For a give-away or golf day, where balls are typically used within a round or a few rounds, that is no cause for concern.

A practical tip: store printed balls dry and out of strong sunlight until you hand them out. That keeps both the white of the ball and the colours of your logo at their best, whether you give them away straight away or hold a little stock for a future event. If you would like to place several brands side by side for a tiered hand-out — Pinnacle for the broad field and a premium ball for your key guests, say — take a look at the applications or discuss your scenario via contact.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order?
The minimum order is 144 golf balls, for example 6 boxes of 24 or 12 dozen. Larger quantities earn tiered discounts.
Do I get a digital proof first?
Yes, always. You receive a digital proof of your logo on the ball and production only starts after your approval.
How long does delivery take?
Allow 5 to 15 working days after you approve the digital proof.
How many colours can I print?
Golf balls are printed in 1 to 5 spot colours. Provide your brand colours as PMS/Pantone values for the best match.
How do I supply my logo?
Preferably as a vector file (EPS, AI or PDF) so the logo comes out perfectly crisp. A high-resolution PNG can work for simple logos.
Can I play with printed golf balls?
Yes. They are original branded balls with a professional print, so they play exactly like unprinted balls.

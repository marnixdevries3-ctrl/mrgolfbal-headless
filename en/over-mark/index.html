Mark Reynolds is a golf professional and the founder of MrGolfbal.nl. Drawing on years of experience on and around the course, he knows which ball suits which golfer — and how to get a logo onto that ball crisply and recognisably.

That combination makes all the difference: you don't just buy printed golf balls, you get advice from someone who knows the game inside out. From the choice between a Pro V1 and Pro V1x to the best printing for your audience.

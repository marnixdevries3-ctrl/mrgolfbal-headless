Golf balls as a corporate gift

A relation who golfs deserves a gift they will actually use. Golf balls with your logo are personal, premium and on target all year — not a drawer-filler, but a present that goes out on the course.

Start printing → Ask Mark for advice

The perfect corporate gift feels personal, stays in use and suits the recipient. For a client or relation who golfs, all three come together in a printed golf ball. Where a bottle of wine is soon gone and a gadget ends up in a drawer, a good golf ball keeps going out on the course month after month — with your logo prominently on show. This page explains how to use golf balls as a considered business gift: from choosing the moment and the brand to personalisation and the request.

MrGolfbal.nl prints exclusively on original Titleist and Pinnacle golf balls with your logo, text or design. You always see a digital proof first, so you know exactly how the gift looks before production begins. And because founder Mark Reynolds is a PGA professional, he helps you choose the ball that suits the player you want to surprise.

Why golf balls make a strong corporate gift

A gift makes an impression when it shows attention. Choose a golf ball and you show that you know what your relation loves to do — and that counts for more than the value of the gift itself. A printed golf ball scores on three points where generic gifts fall short. It is relevant: a golfer always needs balls and will gladly play yours. It is visible: every time the ball sits on the tee, your logo comes back into view. And it is tasteful: on an original Titleist or Pinnacle, a logo radiates quality rather than cheap advertising.

Compare that to the classic pen, power bank or box of chocolates: pleasant, but forgotten before the week is out. A golf ball someone takes to their home course stays in circulation — and your brand literally plays along. Want to finish the gift off? Personalise it with a name or message — see personalise golf balls.

A fitting moment all year round

Unlike a golf day or tournament, a corporate gift is not tied to a single date. Golf balls suit countless moments in relationship management: as an end-of-year gift in December, as a token when signing a contract, as a thank-you after a successful project, or as a welcome present for a new client. That very versatility is what makes printed golf balls so usable in the business calendar.

Because you are not tied to a season, you can have a larger stock printed in one go and hand them out through the year whenever it suits. That is cost-conscious — for larger runs the price per ball falls thanks to tiered pricing — and you always have a polished gift to hand. Record your logo and preferences once, and the next order is quickly arranged.

Which ball suits a premium corporate gift?

For a gift, the impression counts. To pull out the stops for your most important relations, the Titleist Pro V1 is the obvious choice: the most-played ball on tour, recognised worldwide as a benchmark. A golfer knows that name at once and feels taken seriously. With your logo on it, it becomes a gift with status.

Looking for a fine balance between brand experience and budget? The Titleist TruFeel is ideal: the trusted Titleist brand at a more accessible price, pleasant to play for almost any golfer. Handing out to a broader group of relations? The Pinnacle Soft offers an attractive price with a large printing surface. Unsure? Take the golf ball finder or talk it through with Mark about ball choice per recipient.

Personalisation makes the difference

A corporate gift gains value the moment it becomes personal. Alongside your own logo, you can dress the ball with the recipient's name or initials, or a short message such as a thank-you or an anniversary. That turns a set of golf balls from a business token into a present truly made for that one relation.

Printing is done in spot colours, from 1 to 5 colours, so your brand identity comes back sharp and recognisable. Want to give several relations their own name each? That combines well within a single order. Read what is possible on the personalise golf balls page, and see how many colours you can print on a golf ball.

Quantities, packaging and delivery time

The minimum order for printing is 144 golf balls. For corporate gifts, a sleeve of three balls per person is tidy and representative; to be more generous with top relations, give a dozen. With 144 balls you serve, for example, 48 relations with a sleeve, or twelve relations with a full dozen. Calculate your run easily in the configurator or read how many golf balls go in a box or dozen.

Bear the lead time in mind. After supplying your logo you first receive a digital proof; only after your go-ahead does production start, followed by a delivery time of 5 to 15 working days. So plan gifts around the festive season well in advance, as that is a busy moment. For the sharpest result, supply your logo as a vector file (EPS, AI or PDF); read how best to supply your logo.

Packaging and presentation: finishing the gift

A printed golf ball is already spot-on in substance, but the way you present it sets the first impression. A single ball feels like a freebie; a neatly packed sleeve or a full dozen feels like a gift. So choose your format deliberately: three balls in a sleeve are representative and approachable, while a dozen looks complete and ready to use for your top relationships.

To finish it off, add a short, handwritten card with a personal note — that lifts a business gesture straight into something sincere. Hand the gift over in person where you can, at a meeting or dinner; if that is not possible, a smart delivery with a covering note works just as well. We print the balls crisply in one to five spot colours, so your brand reads right down to the detail. Ready to design? Open the configurator.

Concrete moments: when a golf ball lands just right

Because a corporate gift is not tied to a single date, it pays to think ahead about the moments in your relationship management. A few scenarios where a printed ball works remarkably well:

  • Welcoming a new client: a sleeve as an onboarding gesture says "we know you" before the partnership is even up to speed.
  • A contract or anniversary: a full dozen marks a milestone and stays in tangible use.
  • A thank-you after a project: a gesture that is not pushy yet lingers.
  • Year-end: the classic moment — and precisely when a golf ball stands apart from yet another bottle or box.

By having a larger stock printed in one go, you keep a polished gift to hand all year round. Unsure which model suits which relationship? The golf ball selector points the way, or put your question to us via contact.

Building a gift set around the ball

Printed balls stand strong on their own, but they also lend themselves perfectly as the heart of a larger gift. Picture a sleeve combined with other golf accessories into a complete set for the golfing relationship. That way you give not one item but a considered whole, ready to use on the course straight away.

For the ball within such a set: the Titleist Pro V1 gives the set instant status, while the Pinnacle Soft offers fine value when you are giving to a broader group. If you would like inspiration for assembling a bag full of give-aways, look at how a goodie bag is built. And if you are deploying the balls more widely across your business, the golf event page offers further ideas.

With a set, keep an eye on cohesion: a single brand colour recurring on every element turns loose items into a considered whole. And do not overdo the count — a compact, good-quality set reads better than a bag full of frills. The printed ball is and remains the centrepiece; everything else supports it.

The etiquette of corporate gifting: dos and don'ts

A gift can strengthen a relationship or feel awkward — it is in the details. A few guidelines that work well in practice:

  • Do: match the model to how someone actually plays. A keen player values a tour ball; an occasional golfer is just as happy with a more accessible model.
  • Do: keep the message personal and the advertising subtle. A logo that signals quality works better than a pushy slogan.
  • Don't: order at the last minute. Reckon on a delivery time of 5 to 15 working days after proof approval, especially around the festive season.
  • Don't: forget to check whether gifts fit within the recipient's policy; some organisations apply limits to confirm.

For larger quantities, arrange everything neatly via a tailored quote, with volume pricing and a proof schedule that fits your gifting moment.

The value of goodwill that keeps circulating

What makes a golf ball special as a gift is that it is not used up once unwrapped. Where a bottle of wine lasts an evening, a good ball keeps going onto the course month after month — and every time your relationship tees it up, your brand comes back into view, without feeling like advertising. That is exactly the kind of soft, lasting visibility that relationship management is about.

To make that goodwill durable, give with some regularity — a small, recurring gesture builds more than one grand gift.

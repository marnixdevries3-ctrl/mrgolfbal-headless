Printed golf balls for your golf event

A golf event thrives on atmosphere and visibility. Printed golf balls carry your brand across the course throughout the event and go home as a keepsake — from clinic to opening or demo day.

Start printing → Ask Mark for advice

Whether you are organising a clinic, opening a new location or holding a demo day on the course, a golf event is the ideal moment to put your brand in play. Participants spend hours active with a ball in hand, and that ball is exactly what lets you be visible without being pushy. This page explains how to use printed golf balls for a golf event — during the event and long afterwards.

MrGolfbal.nl prints exclusively on original Titleist and Pinnacle golf balls with your logo, text or design. For every event you first receive a digital proof, so you see exactly how the result looks before production starts. Founder Mark Reynolds is a PGA professional and helps you choose the ball that suits the type of event and the participants you expect.

Visibility throughout the whole golf event

At an event, every point of contact counts. A printed golf ball gives you a visibility that, unlike a banner or beach flag, moves with the participant: from the first tee to the last green, your logo is in view again and again. At a clinic or demo day where people point out the ball and photograph it, that effect grows stronger still — your brand literally plays along in the action.

That makes golf balls a natural complement to the rest of your event dressing. Where flags and banners stay in one spot, the ball crosses the course and returns on every hole. Combine that with your other event materials and your brand is present everywhere, without it feeling forced.

Handing out to participants — and a keepsake afterwards

The beauty of a golf event is that the ball works twice. During the event you hand it out to participants to play with; afterwards they take it home and play it on their own course. That keeps your event resonating for weeks after the last putt drops. A participant who finds your ball in their bag is instantly reminded of the day.

Want to give the balls as part of a package? They combine well with other giveaways in a bag — for that approach, take a look at golf balls in a goodie bag. For a sporty, polished whole, a sleeve of three balls per participant is a pleasant standard.

Which ball suits your golf event?

For an event with many participants and mixed levels, you want a ball that plays pleasantly and is keenly priced. The Pinnacle Soft is an excellent choice for that: soft feel, large printing surface and attractive in larger quantities. If you are after a ball with a bit more length and a lively character for an active demo day, the Pinnacle Rush is a sporty option that sits well with enthusiastic players.

Want to give your event a touch of premium with a renowned brand? The Titleist TruFeel offers the trusted Titleist look at an accessible price. Not sure which ball suits your type of event? Take the golf ball finder or put your programme to Mark — he is happy to advise based on the participants.

Your logo sharp on the ball

An event radiates professionalism the moment the details are right, and that starts with a sharply printed logo. Preferably supply your logo as a vector file (EPS, AI or PDF) for the best result. If you only have a JPEG or PNG, Mark will honestly assess whether the quality is sufficient. Printing is done in spot colours, from 1 to 5 colours; provide your brand colours as PMS/Pantone values for an exact match.

Want to know more about preparation? Read how best to supply your logo and how many colours you can print on a golf ball. That way your brand sits flawlessly on the tee during the event.

Quantities and planning for your event

The minimum order for printing is 144 golf balls. Work out how many participants you expect and add a margin for last-minute sign-ups and the organisers. A sleeve of three per participant is tidy; to pull out the stops, give a dozen. Larger runs deliver a more favourable price per ball thanks to tiered pricing — calculate your run in the configurator.

Mind the lead time, certainly in the busy golf season. After you supply your logo, a digital proof comes first; only after your go-ahead does production start, followed by a delivery time of 5 to 15 working days. So lock in your quantities and brand choice well before the event date, so everything is ready in time. Unsure about packaging? Read how many golf balls go in a box or dozen.

How to request your event balls

For a golf event you arrange the order as a business assignment with a tailored quote: no online checkout, but a tidy proposal with tiered pricing, proof planning and a delivery moment matched to your event date. Request your quote via the business quote form or see the options on printing golf balls for businesses. Have a tight timeline or special requirements? Feel free to get in contact — we will look at a workable schedule together.

Planning schedule: fitting golf balls into your event timeline

A golf event lives or dies by its preparation, and printed balls deserve a fixed place in your run sheet. Work backwards from the event date and keep these milestones as a guideline to confirm for your situation:

  • 6–5 weeks out: settle your final participant count, choose your ball and request a quote. Plan for at least 144 balls and add a generous margin for no-shows and last-minute sign-ups.
  • 5–4 weeks out: supply your logo as a vector file and approve the digital proof. Production only starts once you have signed off.
  • 4–2 weeks out: production and delivery, with a lead time of 5 to 15 working days after proof approval.
  • Final week: sort balls by hole or flight, lay sleeves out at the registration desk and set a small reserve aside.

This sequence avoids the classic pitfall of ordering too late in the busy golf season. Work out your run in the configurator or run your timeline past us via contact, and we will check together whether it is feasible.

Using printed balls in your event activations

At a golf event the balls are not just playing kit, they are a way to build memorable moments. A few proven activations that put a printed ball centre stage:

  • Closest to the pin — hand every participant a printed ball on the par 3 and attach a small prize. Your logo sits right on the green during the tensest moment.
  • Longest drive — a logoed sleeve as a reward feels sporty and stays visible in the winner's bag afterwards.
  • Exhibition stand or demo zone — let visitors putt with a printed ball they can keep. A tangible souvenir that makes your stand memorable.
  • Hole-in-one challenge — a ball carrying your brand makes the attempt extra memorable, even when the hole does not drop.

The ball works twice over: as a playing tool during the event and as a keepsake afterwards. If you are running several brands or holes, look at the approach for a sponsor competition or a broadly organised golf tournament.

Combining balls with the rest of your event branding

Printed golf balls come into their own when they are part of a consistent whole. Make sure the colours on the ball align with your banners, tee markers and registration material; supply brand colours as PMS/Pantone values so the spot colours on the ball match the rest exactly. We print in 1 to 5 spot colours, ample for most logos.

Think about the touchpoints around the round too. A printed ball beside a branded tee, a scorecard in your house style and a small thank-you card together create a polished impression that stays with participants. For a business-oriented event this dovetails neatly with a company golf day, where the ball becomes part of a full-day programme. Not sure which ball suits your branding? Try the ball selector.

After the event: keeping the impact visible

The value of a golf event does not stop at the final putt. That is precisely when the printed ball begins its second life: participants play it on their own course, and your logo travels with it. To feed that effect, give a little more than a single ball — a sleeve of three keeps your brand in the bag for weeks afterwards.

Measuring the aftercare is simple: ask afterwards what stuck with people the most, and the printed ball often scores surprisingly high because it is tangible and usable. If you run events regularly, we keep your logo and preferences on file so the next edition is arranged faster. For a recurring corporate programme, request a tailored quote via the business quote form.

Do's and don'ts for event balls

A few practical lessons that make the difference between a ball that stands out and one that goes unnoticed.

  • Do: order generously with a margin, supply your logo as a vector file in good time and review the digital proof carefully for colour and placement.
  • Do: choose the ball that suits your participants' level — a soft, forgiving ball like the Pinnacle Soft works across the board, while the Pinnacle Rush appeals to sportier players.
  • Don't: cram too many colours or fine detail into a small logo; on the curved ball surface a clean, high-contrast mark reads best.
  • Don't: order only a week before the event. Always allow 5 to 15 working days after proof approval.

If you want to go premium, the Titleist TruFeel gives your brand a trusted look at an accessible price. When in doubt, Mark is glad to advise based on your programme.
